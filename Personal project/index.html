Menu




Pizza

Margherita – Classic pizza with tomato sauce, mozzarella cheese, and basil. Pepperoni – Pizza with spicy pepperoni slices and cheese. Hawaiian – Pizza with pineapple and ham. Capricciosa – Pizza with mushrooms, ham, olives, and artichokes.

Classic Burger – Bun, beef patty, lettuce, tomato, cheese. Cheeseburger – Classic burger with extra cheese. Bacon Burger – Burger with crispy bacon. Veggie Burger – Burger made with a vegetable patty. Chicken Burger – Bun with grilled or fried chicken.



Salad
